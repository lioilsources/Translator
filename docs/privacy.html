Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

This policy covers Tlumočník (Interpreter), an app by A+B that lies on the table between two people, recognises who is speaking, translates it and says it aloud to the other person. It is published as com.ol1n.tlumocnik on iOS and Android. There is no account. To translate, the app sends your speech to the developer's translation server, as described below. Separately, it sends anonymous usage statistics, which never contain anything that was said and which you can turn off at any time.

Translation

What the app sends. During a conversation the app sends the microphone audio of what is said (in automatic mode, the stretches the app detects as speech; in push-to-talk mode, while you hold the screen) to the developer's translation server, over an encrypted connection for the servers built into the app, together with the two chosen languages, the conversation mode, whether spoken output is on, which voices were chosen, and this phone's device identifier. The server sends back the recognised text, its translation and the translation as speech.

Device identifier. On first use the app registers this phone with the translation server and receives a random device identifier and an access token. They are used to let the phone in, to count today's translation minutes and to keep the voices recorded on this phone. If you enter an invite code, it is sent to the server to unlock the tester level.

Your own voice. Only if you choose to record one, with your explicit consent, a 30-second recording of your voice and the name you give it are uploaded and stored on the translation server, so translations can be spoken in your voice. This is biometric data. You may record only yourself. Deleting the voice in the app permanently removes the recording from the server and withdraws your consent.

Conversation history (what was said and its translation) is saved only on your phone. It leaves the phone only if you share a conversation yourself, through the system share sheet.

Anonymous Usage Statistics

What is sent. Short records of how the app is used: the app was opened or went to the background, which screen was shown and for how many seconds, a conversation started and how it ended (for example finished, left without a translation, no connection, the server was busy, today's minutes were used up), the two language codes of that conversation, how many seconds it lasted, how long the connection to the translation server took, which features were used for the first time (for example push-to-talk, pause, text only, history, sharing, your own voice), and which kind of error was shown. Each record also carries the app version, platform (iOS or Android), app language, which translation server is in use (by name, not address), and two coarse bands: roughly how many times the app has been used on this device (1, 2, 3–5, 6–10, 11+) and roughly how many days ago (0, 1, 2–6, 7–29, 30+). The full list is published in the app's source repository (docs/telemetry.md).

Never anything that was said. The statistics contain no audio, no recognised or translated text, no voice names, no conversation history and nothing you type. They are separate from translation: they go to a different server and do not use the device identifier or the access token described above.

Why. To see whether new users get to their first translation, where people give up while waiting, which errors they hit and which features are never used, so we can fix them.

No identifiers. The statistics contain no name, no email, no account, no device or advertising identifier and no exact device model. Each session gets a random number that exists only while the app runs, so two sessions cannot be linked to each other or to you. The statistics are not used for advertising or tracking and are not sold or shared.

Who receives them, and where. The statistics are received and stored by the developer, on the developer's own server in the EU (Czech Republic). Nobody else receives them: no analytics company or other third party is involved. Sending them, like any internet connection, reveals your IP address to that server; the IP address is not stored, it is used only to derive your country. Your country is the only location information kept.

How long. Statistics are kept for 12 months and then deleted.

Offline. Without a connection, up to 500 recent records wait on your device and are sent later; older ones are discarded.

How to turn them off. Open Settings (the gear on the main screen) and switch off Anonymous usage statistics (Anonymní statistiky používání in Czech). This takes effect immediately: records waiting on your device are deleted and nothing more is sent, until you turn it back on.

Local Storage

Your choices (languages, mode, voices, server), the device identifier and token, and the conversation history are stored on your phone using standard platform storage. The statistics keep a few small values there — the day measuring began, a session counter and the last day band — from which the bands above are worked out on your device.

Permissions

Microphone: to hear and translate what is said, and to record your own voice if you choose to. The app uses it only while a conversation or a voice recording is running.

Data We Do Not Collect

— Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, account)

— Precise location (only the country, for the statistics)

— The advertising ID or the exact device model

— Contacts, photos, files or anything outside the app

Third-Party Services

Tlumočník contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The anonymous statistics go only to the developer's own server, as described above.

Children

Tlumočník is not directed at children and is not offered in the App Store Kids category or Google Play's Families programme.
